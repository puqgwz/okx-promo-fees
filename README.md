## What does “20% rebate” mean?

A 20% trading-fee rebate does not mean that OKX gives you 20% of your deposit or 20% of every trade’s value.

It normally refers to a portion of eligible trading fees being returned or discounted. For example, if a qualifying trade generates a $10 trading fee and the applicable rebate is 20%, the rebate value would be calculated from the fee amount, not from the full trade size.

The exact calculation can depend on:

- The product being traded
- Your account’s fee tier
- The trading pair
- Whether the order is classified as maker or taker
- The campaign terms attached to your account
- Your location and eligibility
- Whether the rebate is paid directly, issued as a card, or included in a reward package

OKX distinguishes between maker and taker fees. A taker order is generally filled immediately against existing orders in the order book, while a maker order adds liquidity to the order book and may receive a different rate. The fee shown for your account and trading pair is the figure that matters at the time of execution.

## Which OKX products may be relevant to a new user?

The right product depends on what you actually need.

### Spot trading

Spot trading is the simplest route for buying or selling an asset without borrowing or using a derivative contract. It is generally easier to understand than leveraged products, although cryptocurrency prices can still move sharply.

For a new account created with CASH20, spot trading is the most straightforward place to check how the referral rebate applies. Look at the order preview and fee line before confirming a transaction.

### Convert

Convert is designed for direct asset swaps. OKX’s interface describes Convert as a quick conversion route with no separate trading fee, but the quoted price and spread still matter. A “zero trading fee” label should not automatically be read as “the cheapest possible execution.” Compare the quoted amount with an order-book trade before converting a large amount.

The New User Full Rebate Program also lists zero-fee Convert transactions among excluded transactions for its first qualifying trade, so Convert may not count toward a campaign requirement.

### Margin trading

Margin trading involves borrowed funds and interest. It can increase exposure, but it also increases the speed at which losses can accumulate. If you are only looking for an OKX promo code, there is no reason to use margin simply to activate a reward.

### Futures and perpetuals

Perpetual futures track the price of an underlying asset without a fixed expiry date. They use margin and may involve funding payments between long and short positions. Liquidation is possible if the account no longer meets maintenance-margin requirements.

A referral rebate can reduce some eligible trading costs, but it does not protect a position from liquidation and does not compensate for a losing trade.

### Options

Options are more complex than ordinary spot purchases because the value depends on factors such as the underlying price, strike price, time, and volatility. Confirm that options are available in your region and understand the contract mechanics before treating a promotional reward as a reason to trade them.

## How to reduce your total OKX trading costs

The promo code is only one part of the calculation.

### Use the fee shown for your account

Logged-out fee tables are useful for orientation, but the rate shown after login is more relevant because it reflects your current tier and region. OKX says users can check their fee tier and current trading rates from the account area or app.

### Understand maker and taker orders

A market order usually executes immediately and is generally treated as a taker order. A limit order can become a maker order if it rests on the order book rather than matching immediately.

That does not mean every limit order is automatically cheaper. A limit order can still execute as a taker if it immediately matches available liquidity.

### Check spread and execution price

Trading fees are only one part of the cost. The spread between the buy and sell price can matter, particularly on less liquid pairs or when using a direct conversion flow.

### Avoid unnecessary turnover

A rebate calculated on trading fees can still leave you paying more if you make trades you did not otherwise need. The most economical trade is often the one you do not place.

### Watch funding and liquidation-related costs

For perpetual futures, funding fees are separate from ordinary maker and taker fees. Liquidation can also involve charges at the applicable taker rate. OKX’s fee documentation separates these costs from regular order-book trading fees.
